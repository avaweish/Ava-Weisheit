About

Hi, I'm Ava.

I'm a Data Science major at Indiana University with a minor in Statistics and Sports Marketing and Management. I'm deeply interested in the intersection of analytics, sports, and performance - using data to uncover the stories and strategies that make teams and organizations stronger.

My coursework and projects have given me hands-on experience in Python, R, and data visualization. Outside the classroom, I work as a manager at IU's campus box office, where I've picked up real experience in operations, customer service, and leading a team. I'm also active in the Women in Technology Club and IU Women's Basketball Club, stay involved in my sorority, Alpha Delta Pi, and volunteer regularly at the Bloomington Animal Shelter. When I'm not working or in class, you'll find me hiking, playing basketball, going on adventures with friends, or mentoring young athletes through camps.

I'm eager to bring my skills into a collaborative, forward-thinking environment where I can keep learning and contribute to meaningful projects.
