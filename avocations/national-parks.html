National Parks Passport

One of my biggest personal goals is to visit every national park and ranger station in the country, collecting a stamp and a pin from each stop along the way in my National Parks passport book. I'm also trying to hike as many trails as I can at every park I visit, and once I have the gear, I'd like to get into climbing too.
